🛡 The wall is real

236.1 total yards allowed per game — #1 in the NFL — and just 4.00 yards per play, also #1. Opponents average 15.1 first downs per game, fewest in the league. This is not a bend-don't-break unit; it barely bends.

✈️ No-fly zone

149.4 net passing yards allowed per game (#1) with an opponent passer rating of 76.7 (#1). Seattle rushes four, rarely blitzes (24.1%, 25th), and still generates pressure on 30.2% of dropbacks (#2) — and when it does blitz, opponents manage −0.25 EPA per dropback (2nd).

⚠️ The crack in the wall

Once opponents reach the red zone, the magic fades: 69.6% of trips end in touchdowns (#29), and on 4th down opponents convert 85.7% (31st). The defense dominates between the 20s — then gives it back inside them.
